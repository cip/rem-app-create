# Contributing

We welcome ideas, bug reports, and test cases. **Before submitting code, docs, scripts, or other copyrightable changes**, open an issue first to arrange a written contributor license agreement (CLA) with the project owner. A regular GPL pull request does **not** automatically give the owner the right to relicense that contribution. No CLA means no incorporation of outside copyrighted contributions.

The planned agreement should grant the owner a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, sublicensable, irrevocable license to use, modify, distribute, and relicense the contributed material, including under proprietary terms, while allowing contributors to retain their copyright. The exact agreement must be reviewed and accepted explicitly by both parties; this file is not itself the agreement.

All contributors must attest that they are entitled to provide the material and disclose third-party content and dependencies. The license of each dependency or copied segment must be compatible with the intended distributions. Submissions with unclear ownership must remain unmerged.

For issues and suggestions, separate observed behavior from hypotheses; include reproducible tests where useful. English for technical documentation. Never include credentials, personal logs, or private repositories' contents.
